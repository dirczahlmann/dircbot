# Business Building & Scaling

How Dirc built 8 unicorns. Use these for business, leadership, scaling questions.

## The 4 Stages of Building a Unicorn

**Stage 1 — Proof of Concept (€0–€100K MRR)**
- Founder sells every deal personally
- No team, no automation, no systems
- Goal: prove the offer works
- Time: 6–18 months

**Stage 2 — Repeatability (€100K–€1M MRR)**
- Build the playbook
- First sales hires (2–5 people)
- Document everything that works
- Goal: someone other than the founder can close
- Time: 12–24 months

**Stage 3 — Scale Engine (€1M–€10M MRR)**
- Marketing engine + sales engine running independently
- Operational systems built
- Mid-management layer
- Goal: predictable monthly growth
- Time: 24–36 months

**Stage 4 — Unicorn Trajectory (€10M+ MRR)**
- Multi-region, multi-product
- Brand becomes the moat
- Capital strategy: when to raise, when to bootstrap
- Goal: market category leadership
- Time: 36+ months

**Most companies fail moving from Stage 1 to Stage 2.** They try to scale before they've made it repeatable.

## Team Building — The Outcome Rule

Build a team that owns **outcomes**, not tasks.

- A task-owner does what you tell them. They scale linearly with your time.
- An outcome-owner makes decisions to hit a target. They scale exponentially.

**Hiring rule:** Don't hire someone who needs to be told what to do. Hire someone who tells YOU what they're going to do — and then does it.

## The 90-Day Sprint Framework

Every quarter, run a 90-day sprint:

- **Days 1–7:** Set ONE major outcome for the quarter. Just one. Write it down.
- **Days 8–30:** Identify and fix the bottleneck preventing that outcome.
- **Days 31–60:** Execute the core work. Measure weekly.
- **Days 61–90:** Optimize, document, and prepare the next sprint.

**Most people set 5 goals per quarter. They hit zero of them.** Set one. Hit it. Move.

## Cashflow Discipline

The unicorn rule that nobody teaches:
- **Cashflow before growth.** Always.
- **30% rule:** Always have 30% operating cash buffer
- **Pay-yourself-first:** Founder's salary is fixed and protected, even in growth mode
- **Burn-rate awareness:** Every founder should know their daily burn rate by heart

## Network as Moat

Dirc's strongest asset is his global network. Bankers. Entrepreneurs. Investors. Government advisors.

**The principle:** Your network is not who you know. It's who picks up your call.

**Build it by:**
1. Give before you ask. 100 times. Then ask once.
2. Always over-deliver on introductions you make.
3. Stay close to your top 50 contacts — one substantive touch per quarter.
4. Be the connector. Connect 2 people every week.

That's how I built a network across 50+ countries.

## The 1 Question Test

Before any major business decision, ask:
**"Does this move me closer to or further from the kind of business I actually want to run in 5 years?"**

Most people optimize for the next month. Unicorns get built by people who optimize for the next 5 years.
