# Sales Frameworks

Dirc's tested sales playbook. Use these patterns when answering sales questions.

## Objection Handling — The 3-Step Pattern

When prospect raises any objection (price, timing, "thinking about it"):

**Step 1 — AGREE.** "Smart, you should think about it." or "You're right, this isn't cheap."
*Rationale: Agreement disarms. They expect you to push back. When you don't, the defense drops.*

**Step 2 — ISOLATE.** "What specifically — the price, the timing, or whether it actually works?"
*Rationale: "Need to think about it" is never the real objection. Isolating exposes the real one.*

**Step 3 — CLOSE ON THE REAL OBJECTION.** Whatever they isolate, that's the only objection that matters.
*Rationale: You can't close a smokescreen. You can only close the real concern.*

**Proof:** "I closed nine-figure deals in 50 countries with this exact sequence."

## The 3-List Recruiting Method

For team building or business launching:

- **List 1:** 20 people who already trust you (warm market)
- **List 2:** 20 people who already buy what you sell (qualified market)
- **List 3:** 20 people who already lead teams (high-leverage market)

**Rule:** Don't pitch. Ask ONE question per list:
- List 1: "Would you give me 10 minutes of honest feedback?"
- List 2: "What would make you switch?"
- List 3: "Who else should know about this?"

This is how 90% of network marketers fail — they pitch friends and family. This is how the 10% win.

## The 4-Bottleneck Audit (Scaling)

Most people try to scale what they have. Wrong move. Before scaling, find your bottleneck:

1. **Leads** — not enough top-of-funnel?
2. **Conversion** — leads come in but don't buy?
3. **Delivery** — customers buy but churn?
4. **Retention** — customers stay but don't expand?

You can only fix ONE at a time. Identify which one is leaking the most revenue. Fix that. Then move to the next.

**Dirc's rule:** "Scaling chaos creates bigger chaos. Systemize before you scale."

## The Cash-Flow-Before-Growth Principle

Never scale on credit. Never scale on hype. Never scale on next month's projected revenue.

**Rule:** Cash flow before growth. Always. Every unicorn I built ran on this principle.

## 7 Hooks That Work in 2026

1. **Pattern Interrupt:** "Here's what 99% of [niche] gets wrong..."
2. **Wrong Question / Right Question:** "You're asking X. That's the wrong question. The right question is Y."
3. **Insider Reveal:** "I've spent 14 years in [field]. Here's what nobody tells you..."
4. **Counterintuitive Truth:** "Everyone says do X. I did the opposite. Here's why it worked."
5. **Specific Number:** "How I went from $0 to $X in [time] — the exact 3 steps."
6. **Mistake Confession:** "I lost $X learning this. You don't have to."
7. **Authority Frame:** "After mentoring 1.5M entrepreneurs, here's the only thing that matters."
